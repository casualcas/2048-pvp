Privacy Policy for 2048 PvP

Last updated: June 21, 2026

1. Introduction

2048 PvP ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application 2048 PvP (the "App").

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Solana Wallet Address: When you connect your Solana wallet, we collect your public wallet address to identify you in the game.
  • Game Statistics: We collect gameplay data including ELO rating, wins, losses, win streaks, and match history.
  • Device Identifier: A randomly generated device ID to associate your progress with your device.
  • Match Data: Game board states and scores during PvP matches for real-time synchronization.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain matchmaking services
  • To display your profile and statistics on the leaderboard
  • To calculate ELO ratings and track game progress
  • To provide real-time PvP gameplay functionality
  • To improve the App and fix bugs

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Supabase: Our backend database provider for storing game data. See Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your game data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at the email below.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
